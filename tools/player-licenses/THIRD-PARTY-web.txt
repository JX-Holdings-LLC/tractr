Third-party software in the Tractr player
====================================================

This game runs on farm-player, the Tractr game player. The player
includes the open-source Rust crates listed below. Their licenses follow. The
source code of each crate is available from crates.io
(https://crates.io/crates/<name>/<version>).

The player also embeds the Inter and Atkinson Hyperlegible typefaces (Regular
and Bold) under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, and a plugin sandbox built from
QuickJS (quickjs-ng), rquickjs-sys and wasi-libc (with parts of musl and
cloudlibc); their licenses are at the end of this file. farm-player itself is
Tractr's own code, under the Business Source License 1.1 and the MIT License, which comes last.
